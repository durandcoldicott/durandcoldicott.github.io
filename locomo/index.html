    01 — The ecosystem

    Locomo doesn't sell to one customer.

    The person using a Locomo product is often not the person researching, recommending, or paying for it. That's not a guess — Locomo's own site and a public case study both confirm it.

    VERIFIED

    VERIFIED

    Locomo's refund policy names purchases "paid for directly by a plan manager" — some sales run through third-party disability-support funding, not just direct payment.

    VERIFIED
