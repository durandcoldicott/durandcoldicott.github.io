Install Panana

Put Panana on your home screen so it opens like an app — full screen, works offline, and (on iPhone) can send practice reminders.

iPhone or iPad — Safari

  1. Open durand.nz/panana in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button — at the bottom of the screen (or top-right on iPad). If you can't see it, scroll up a little so the toolbar appears.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen . Not in the list? Tap Edit Actions… at the bottom and add it.
  4. Check the name says Panana, then tap Add (top-right).
  5. Find the 🍌 Panana icon on your home screen and open it from there.

🔔 Reminders on iPhone only work when Panana is opened from the home-screen icon (iOS 16.4 or later). Then go to Ētahi atu → Reminders and tap Allow.

iPhone or iPad — Chrome

  1. Open durand.nz/panana in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Share button in the address bar (top-right).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen (you may need to tap More to see it), then Add.
  4. Open Panana from the new 🍌 icon on your home screen.

Don't see Add to Home Screen? Your iPhone may need updating (iOS 16.4+). Safari always works — copy the link into Safari and follow the Safari steps.

Android — Chrome

  1. Open durand.nz/panana in Chrome.
  2. If a bar pops up saying Install Panana or Add Panana to Home screen, tap it — you're done. Otherwise…
  3. Tap the menu button (three dots, top-right).
  4. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then Install / Add.
  5. Open Panana from the 🍌 icon on your home screen or in your app drawer.

🔔 Reminders work on Android in Chrome — open Ētahi atu → Reminders and tap Allow.

Android — Samsung Internet

  1. Open durand.nz/panana in Samsung Internet.
  2. Tap the menu button (three lines, bottom-right).
  3. Tap Add page to → Home screen (or Install if you see it).
  4. Tap Add, then open Panana from the 🍌 icon.

After installing

  1. ✅ Sign in once from the home-screen icon — you'll stay signed in.
  2. 🔔 Turn on reminders in Ētahi atu → Reminders if you'd like a daily nudge.
  3. 📶 Works offline — your progress saves on your phone and syncs when you're back online.
  4. 🔄 Updates arrive automatically — if something looks old, close Panana fully and open it again.
