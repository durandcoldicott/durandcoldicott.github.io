Your privacy

The short version: Panana keeps only what it needs to save your progress. No email, no ads, no selling data.

What we store

  • Your account — the name you chose, your username and the date you joined. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read it, including the organiser.
  • Your progress — the words you've learned, your answers' history, streak, XP, goals and app settings.
  • Reminders (only if you turn them on) — your reminder time, time zone and a code from your phone that lets us send the notification.
  • Feedback you send, and which question it was about.
  • Your rōpū — which groups you're in, and the name and invite code of any you start.
  • Sign-in — a cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 90 days, and short-lived counts of failed sign-ins (cleared after 15 minutes) to stop password guessing.

We don't ask for your email, phone number or location, and Panana doesn't add analytics or advertising trackers.

Who can see it

  • People in your rōpū (groups you join) see your name, streak and XP on that rōpū's leaderboard. The Everyone board shows the same to all members. Turn both off in Ētahi atu → Show me on leaderboards.
  • A rōpū's owner sees the usernames of people in their rōpū and can remove someone from it. They can't see your password, progress details or other rōpū.
  • The site admin can see the members list (names, usernames, when you joined, last active, streak and XP), every rōpū, and the feedback you send. They can reset your password or remove your account or a rōpū. They can't see your password.
  • Nobody else. We don't share or sell your data.

Where it's kept

On Cloudflare, which runs the website and stores the data on its global network (so it may be held outside Aotearoa). A copy of your progress is also saved on your own device so Panana works offline. Reminders are delivered through Apple's or Google's notification service.

How long

Until you delete your account or an admin removes it. Deleting is immediate and removes your account, progress, reminders and feedback.

Your choices

  • See your data — Ētahi atu → Export backup downloads everything about your progress.
  • Fix it — ask the organiser to change your name, or use the feedback form.
  • Delete it — Ētahi atu → Delete my account.
  • Lost your phone? Change your password or tap Sign out other devices in Ētahi atu.

These match your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. Panana is a small, free, non-commercial app for our class, run by the group organiser at durand.nz — get in touch there or through the feedback form with any questions.
